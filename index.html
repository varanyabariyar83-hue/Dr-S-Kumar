Dental implants

Lost a tooth? Get one back that stays put.

An implant replaces the root of the missing tooth. Once it bonds with the jaw, it holds a crown you can brush, chew and smile with like a natural tooth.

CrownThe visible tooth, matched to your teeth's shade.
AbutmentThe connector between crown and implant.
Titanium implantA small screw in the jaw that acts as the new root.

Questions

Patients often ask

Is root canal treatment painful?

With good local anaesthesia, a root canal feels much like getting a filling. The tooth may feel tender for a day or two afterwards.

How long does a dental implant take?

Placing the implant takes one short sitting. The jaw then needs a few weeks to bond with it, after which the final crown is fitted. Dr. Kumar will give you a timeline after checking your X-ray.

When should my child first see a dentist?

Around the time the first tooth appears, or by the first birthday. Early visits catch cavities in milk teeth and help children feel comfortable.

Am I too old for braces?

No. Adults can straighten their teeth too, and ceramic braces or clear aligners are less visible options.

Do I need an appointment?

Booking is recommended so you are seen on time. For tooth pain or an injury, call us and we will see you as early as possible.
